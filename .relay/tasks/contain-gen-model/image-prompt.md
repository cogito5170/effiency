# Prompt

Create exactly one portrait photograph, 1024 x 1536 pixels. Editorial museum photography, convincingly real, with the crisp detail and tonal depth of a medium-format camera.

In a dark museum gallery, photograph a single small architectural grid-frame model standing alone on a low matte charcoal-black rectangular plinth. The model is a freestanding, coherent lattice skeleton made from thin warm pale timber members: orderly square cells, straight posts and crossbeams, with a shallow second plane of framing visible through the front to establish physical depth. Its restrained geometry evokes the exposed timber structure of a hanok courtyard house and a display-shelf grid without becoming a furnished house or a collection of separate objects. Keep the cells mostly empty. One small muted brick-red inset in a single lower cell is the only colored accent. Show fine natural wood grain, subtly varied timber tone, precise fitted joints and believable handmade construction, with high-resolution joint detail.

Centered, almost perfectly frontal camera at the model's mid-height, straight verticals, minimal perspective distortion, normal-to-short-telephoto lens. The complete model forms one immediately legible silhouette at thumbnail size and fills most of the portrait vertically. Tight, decisive editorial framing: a modest breathing space above, narrow clean black margins on both sides, and the low plinth close to the bottom edge. Preserve the model's outer edges. There is enough subtle depth to read the skeleton as a physical museum object, while the frontal square grid remains dominant. Deep focus across the timber and joints; no distracting blurred foreground.

A single soft museum spotlight from overhead gently reveals the upper edges, front faces, grain and joints, with deep natural shadows inside the cells and beneath the structure. Controlled exposure, soft highlights, no glare or blown-out wood, no visible light fixture. Quiet, contemplative, refined atmosphere. Warm pale wood against a near-black background; muted red limited to the one small inset; dark neutral plinth. Gallery walls and floor disappear into an even near-black field approximating RGB (4, 5, 5), especially at every image edge, to blend into a black magazine page.

This photograph will be placed inside the word Contain in an editorial layout: prioritize one strong readable object and uncluttered structure, but do not render any letters, typography, magazine page, or word-shaped mask in the image itself. Photographic realism throughout, restrained museum documentation rather than illustration. No people, text, labels, logos, watermarks, other exhibits, furniture, busy background, CGI or 3D-render appearance, decorative borders, vignette frames, or artificial edge-darkening effects.
